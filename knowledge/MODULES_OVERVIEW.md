# Claude Code for Non-Coders: Module Overview

This document provides a detailed breakdown of the curriculum for "Claude Code for Non-Coders." It serves as a shared knowledge base to ensure a consistent understanding of the concepts, tools, and objectives covered in each module.

---

## Module 01: Why Chat AI Is Not Enough
**Core Concept:** Moving from "Chatting" to "Systems."

### Key Themes:
*   **The Cold Start Problem:** Every new chat window starts with zero context. You have to re-explain your company, your tone, your clients, and your rules every time.
*   **Inconsistency Demo:** Comparing two windows with the same brief and prompt results in different structures, tones, and quality.
    *   *Window 1:* Formal, invented pricing table, 3-phase structure.
    *   *Window 2:* Casual, emojis, bullet points, different budget split.
*   **The Problem with "No Context":** Without a system, you spend 45+ minutes editing AI output before it's usable. Next week, you start from zero again.
*   **The Solution:** A **System** that provides context, rules, templates, and QA.

---

## Module 02: Your AI Workflow Workspace
**Core Concept:** Building a workspace that "knows who you are."

### Key Themes:
*   **Environment Setup:** Transitioning from the web browser to a local terminal-based workflow (Claude Code).
*   **Context Persistence:** Using files like `CLAUDE.md` and `README.md` to store persistent context about the project, the user, and the desired output standards.
*   **The Goal:** A workspace where the AI doesn't just respond to prompts but understands the broader project environment.

---

## Module 03: Building Your First Workflow
**Core Concept:** Executing a complete, end-to-end workflow.

### Key Themes:
*   **Workflow Execution:** Moving beyond single prompts to multi-step processes.
*   **Consistency:** Ensuring that the same input consistently produces the same high-quality output by following a defined system.
*   **Efficiency:** Reducing the "human-in-the-loop" effort by automating the repetitive parts of the context-setting and formatting.

---

## Module 04: AI Roles, Skills & Advanced Design
**Core Concept:** Scaling and customizing your AI assistance.

### Key Themes:
*   **AI Roles:** Writing custom role files to define specific personas and expertise for different tasks.
*   **Slash Commands:** Designing custom commands to trigger specific workflows or actions quickly.
*   **Knowledge Bases:** Building a repo-based knowledge base that accumulates intelligence and gets smarter with every use.
*   **Scaling:** Moving from one workflow to a suite of custom agents tailored to your specific business needs.
